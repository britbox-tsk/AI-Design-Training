# Learning Log

**This is for you.** Nobody reviews it, collects it, or measures it. It's a place to jot down what you learned so it sticks, and to notice what's working for you. Skip it entirely if it isn't useful, or fill in only the parts you like.

Copy this file (or paste it into your notes app) and keep one entry per module or session. Two minutes is plenty.

## Optional reflection prompts

Use these whenever you feel like writing a bit more. Pick one, not all.

- What did Claude get right that surprised me?
- Where did Claude get it wrong, and how did I notice?
- What would have taken me much longer in Figma alone? What would have been faster in Figma?
- What did I learn about how code works that I didn't know before?
- Which of my design instincts helped most when working with Claude?
- What would I tell someone starting this module tomorrow?

## Checkpoint (optional, every few modules)

- What can I do now that I couldn't a few weeks ago?
- What still feels out of reach, and is it something I want to learn?
- Do I want to go deeper, move on, or take a break?
- Is there something I'd like help with, such as pairing with an engineer or a question for the team channel?

## Sharing (entirely up to you)

If something in here would help others, such as a good prompt, a gotcha, or a neat result, consider posting it in the team channel. Share a screenshot and a sentence. You never have to share the log itself.
